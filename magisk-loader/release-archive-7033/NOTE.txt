7033 (v1.9.2.9) — why this directory has two riru zips
======================================================

*.as-flashed.zip
    The bytes that were actually installed on Z11_SN and used for the on-device
    BUG-007 measurements (app bar 166->97px, content 163->288px, nav 139->83px,
    fab/switch overlap replaced by a 49px gap) and for screenshots 12-15.
    Recovered from /data/local/tmp/lsposed-7033.zip on the device after the
    release/ copy had already been overwritten. Its manager.apk hashes
    92f803c936f891b25286e21ce74aace6bc9c021e05a0bab1c39cca52c3d7d6f3, identical
    to /data/adb/modules/riru_lsposed/manager.apk, so this is provably the build
    those results came from.

*.rebuilt-never-flashed.zip  (and the matching manifest / sha256)
    A later build of the same version constants carrying the collapsing-toolbar
    title fix. It overwrote the original 7033 zips in magisk-loader/release/
    before they were archived — the mistake BUG-009 is about, made in the small:
    two different artifacts claiming one identity. It was never flashed and never
    handed to anyone. Its content is superseded by 7034, which carries the same
    resource change under an identity of its own; it is kept only so the byte
    history is complete.

    The zygisk counterpart of the as-flashed 7033 is NOT recoverable — only the
    riru zip was ever pushed to the device. Nothing was verified on zygisk 7033,
    so no result depends on those bytes.
